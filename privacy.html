Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This policy covers the Tinterlude mobile app, published by Backchannel Tackle LLC under the Backchannel Apps name, and this information website.

The short version

Tinterlude does not require an app account and does not include advertising or analytics. We do not operate a server that collects your artwork or monitors how you color. Coloring works offline; store purchases, restoring purchases, and subscription checks use Apple or Google services.

What stays on your device

Your artwork, custom palettes, preferences, coloring progress, streak and reminder settings are stored locally on your device. The app also stores Premium status and related subscription information locally so it can unlock purchased features.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple App Store or Google Play processes subscriptions and payments. Through the store’s billing interface, the app accesses purchase and entitlement information such as product identifiers, transaction information, subscription status and relevant dates to unlock Premium and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple and Google process information under their own privacy policies.

Apple privacy policy · Google privacy policy

Reminders and permissions

If enabled, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in the app or your device settings. Saving exported artwork to your photo library may require permission; this is used when you choose to save an image.

Sharing artwork

When you export or share artwork, you choose the destination through your device’s sharing tools. The app or service you choose then handles that image under its own policies.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond and resolve your request, retaining them as needed for support and applicable obligations. Please avoid including payment details or other sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you erase it through the app or remove the app’s data. Exported images, email correspondence and any device or cloud backups are separate and may remain until you delete them through those services. Erasing artwork or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription; cancel it through Apple or Google.

Children

The app does not ask users to provide personal information or create accounts. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact us so we can address it.

This website

This site uses no added analytics, advertising scripts, contact forms or tracking cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure the hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact

Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, email apps@backchanneltackle.com.
